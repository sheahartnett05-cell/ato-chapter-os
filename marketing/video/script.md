# Chaptera product video script

Brand: **Chaptera** · URL: **chapteramanager.com** · Demo only (guest preview).

## Walkthrough (~2:15)

### 1. Hook (0:00–0:08)
**VO:** Your chapter runs on GroupMe, five spreadsheets, and a prayer. Chaptera puts it in one place.

### 2. Officer Home (0:08–0:22)
**VO:** Every officer gets a home built for their job, plus a ledger of what needs attention today.

### 3. Calendar and events (0:22–0:38)
**VO:** Events, RSVPs, and required meetings, in one calendar the whole chapter actually checks.

### 4. Attendance and excuses (0:38–0:55)
**VO:** Take attendance in seconds. Excuses are submitted, approved, and counted automatically.

### 5. Dues (0:55–1:10)
**VO:** Know who's paid without chasing a spreadsheet.

### 6. Recruitment (1:10–1:32)
**VO:** Run rush from first contact to bid day, and new members land on the roster automatically.

### 7. Announcements (1:32–1:45)
**VO:** Announcements, polls, and sign-ups that don't get buried.

### 8. Chapter Room — mobile (1:45–1:58)
**VO:** Members get a clean app on their phone, with just what they need.

### 9. Chapter Setup (1:58–2:10)
**VO:** Turn modules on or off and assign officer seats. It fits how your chapter works.

### 10. Close (2:10–2:20)
**VO:** Try the live demo. No account needed. chapteramanager.com

## 30s ad
1. Hook (0–2s): Spreadsheets → one home.

4. Beat: Mobile Chapter Room.
